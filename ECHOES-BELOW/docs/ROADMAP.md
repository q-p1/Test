# ECHOES BELOW — Master Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Creative Bible
Status: locked enough to build.

## Phase 1 — Visual Identity Lock
Finalize protagonist/supporting-character references, Greyhaven environment language, lighting/material direction, and responsive UI system.

## Phase 2 — Chapter 1 Vertical Slice
This is the only active production phase.

Milestones:
1. Real Unity project launches into gameplay.
2. Third-person player movement and camera feel production-ready.
3. One authored Greyhaven Central route with collisions, interactions, and environmental storytelling.
4. Full Chapter 1 progression from intended beginning to intended ending.
5. Dialogue, subtitles, story triggers, and cinematic transitions.
6. Investigation/puzzle loop and acoustic-analysis gameplay.
7. Tension/stealth encounter logic and required AI behavior.
8. Inventory/resources plus checkpoint/save/continue.
9. Visual, lighting, audio, UI, accessibility, keyboard/mouse, and controller polish.
10. Full end-to-end QA with no known progression-blocking bugs or softlocks.

## Phase 3 — Core Systems Lock
Only after Chapter 1 passes the completion gate. Harden reusable player, camera, interaction, inventory, AI, save, localization, audio, and accessibility systems.

## Phase 4 — Chapters 2–3
First campaign expansion.

## Phase 5 — Chapters 4–6
Mid-game expansion and system combinations.

## Phase 6 — Chapters 7–8
Late-game content and transformations.

## Phase 7 — Chapters 9–10
Campaign payoff and conclusion.

## Phase 8 — Cinematic / Character Pass
Animation, blocking, transitions, performance, walk-and-talk, contextual reactions.

## Phase 9 — Audio Pass
Ambience, Foley, room acoustics, voice, music, dynamic silence.

## Phase 10 — Environment Art Pass
Replace placeholders and complete authored world detail.

## Phase 11 — Full Campaign QA
Two full campaign passes minimum, including alternate orders and edge cases.

## Phase 12 — Optimization / Platform Pass
PC primary. Controller support required. iPad/iPhone secondary profiles where practical.

## Phase 13 — Release Candidate
No new features. Bug fixing, performance, presentation, accessibility, packaging.

## Non-negotiables
- No procedural-feeling filler.
- No filler chapters.
- No feature creep before the current gate is passed.
- No counting progress from prompts or plans.
- No story spoilers in progress reports.
- No moving to Chapters 2–10 before Chapter 1 is verified.
